Privacy Policy — Jack's Desk Widget

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission via Google sign-in, the app reads events from your Google Calendar (read-only) and reads and updates tasks in your Google Tasks lists.

How data is used

Calendar and task data is used only to display it in the widget on your own computer and to sync changes you make (adding or completing tasks) back to Google Tasks.

Storage and sharing

No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs. Nothing is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. Your sign-in token is stored locally on your device and you can revoke access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
